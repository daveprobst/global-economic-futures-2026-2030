07 / MODEL RISK

Honest uncertainty

The model has signal.
It does not have certainty.

The historical model modestly improves Brier score against the base rate while its log score is nearly unchanged. Tail subclasses use transparent structural calibration. Every number should be read as conditional on this architecture.
